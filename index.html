About

I’m an Engineering Manager with a hands-on technical background and a strong bias toward execution. I specialize in building high-performing teams, scaling distributed systems, implementing agile methodologies, and delivering reliable products in data-rich and media-focused environments.

I have led engineers across organizations such as Disney, Amazon, and NBCUniversal, partnering closely with product, data, and business stakeholders to deliver platforms used by millions. I have left my mark on products in web applications, streaming services, and mobile applications. My mission is to empower my teams to craft innovative solutions that cater to the diverse and global audiences we serve.

Colleagues know me as a highly creative professional who delivers innovative, simple solutions to complex business problems. I constantly learn about new technology and continue studying to better myself and my teams.
